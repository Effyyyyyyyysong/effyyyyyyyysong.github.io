How I work

Growth is a system,
not a guess.

Every market I've worked in starts with the same question: where is the real opportunity? I answer it with data, then build the infrastructure to capture it at scale.

Step 01
Find the signal
Who is worth investing in — and why?
👥 Population segmentation
White-box cohort design using GMV output, behavioural signals, posting frequency, and unit economics. Every segment has an explicit business rationale.
📡 Behavioural signal analysis
Page-visit frequency, engagement patterns, and action triggers to identify incentive-sensitive vs incentive-resistant users before spending.
🔍 Market insight & competitive analysis
Systematic monitoring of competitor activities and market whitespace to surface growth opportunities and inform strategic prioritisation.
Step 02
Build the system
How do you design it — before spending a penny?
🏗️ Incentive & loyalty architecture
Percentile-based numerical strategy, completion rate prediction, tier design, and loyalty mechanics. Pre-analysis revenue modelling across variants before any budget is committed.
✉️ CRM & lifecycle systems
Segmentation-driven CRM across in-app, push, and email. Lifecycle journeys for activation, retention, and reactivation — measured by incremental uplift, not vanity opens.
🧪 Experimentation framework
A/B testing with holdout groups across incentive amount, type, and time window. Measures true incremental uplift — not just correlation.
💰 Monetisation design
End-to-end variable system design across e-commerce (commission, flat fee) and live commerce (gifting, subscription, community). Conversion pathways from engagement → spend.
Step 03
Scale the market
How do you make it repeatable across markets?
🌍 Multi-market GTM rollout
Coordinated execution across 11+ markets in EMEA and SEA — aligning Product, Data, Ops, and local market leads on priorities, sequencing, and OKRs.
📊 OKR & planning cycles
Annual and quarterly planning cadence, QBR design, budget tracking, and executive reporting. Translating strategy into operational plans that teams actually execute.
🔄 ROI iteration
Three-dimensional optimisation: actionability → incremental GMV → cost-per-incremental-unit. Post-analysis feeds directly into the next planning cycle.
